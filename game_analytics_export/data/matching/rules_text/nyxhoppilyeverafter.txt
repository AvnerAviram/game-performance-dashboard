Hoppily Ever After Payouts Only the highest win is paid per winning combination. Winning combinations consist of one symbol from each adjacent reel, starting from the leftmost reel. Matching symbols on the same reel do not form a single combination but may create separate wins. Scatter wins are added to ANY WAY payouts. Wild Symbol The Wild symbol substitutes for all symbols except Purple Egg, Blue Egg, Pink Egg, Yellow Egg, and all jackpot symbols. Wild symbols appear only on reels 2, 3, 4, and 5. Cash Falls™ Feature During the base game, egg symbols display multiplier values based on their reel position. Reel Egg Values and Awards 1 & 5 0.5x – 10x total bet (Purple Egg) 2 & 4 0.5x – 25x total bet (Purple, Blue, Yellow, Pink Eggs) 3 0.5x – 100x total bet (Purple, Blue, Yellow, Pink Eggs) If an egg lands on a reel, all eggs on that reel remain locked for the next three spins at the same bet level. The number of remaining spins is displayed below the reel. If all positions on a reel are filled with eggs, the Cash Falls™ Feature is triggered, awarding all displayed values on that reel. After the feature is awarded, the eggs on that reel are cleared. If special eggs (Blue, Yellow, Pink) are present, their associated free spin features are also triggered. Symbols hidden behind eggs are not evaluated. All Cash Falls™ Feature rules apply to the base game. Feature Eggs Blue, Yellow, and Pink Eggs behave like Purple Eggs and contribute to the Cash Falls™ Feature. These eggs may trigger free spins when they land or while they remain locked. If included in a Cash Falls™ Feature, they also award their associated free spins. Feature eggs remain on reels for three spins and may trigger their features multiple times. More Free Spins Feature Triggered by Blue Eggs. Awards between 9 and 40 free spins. If not triggered fully, a minimum of 7 free spins is awarded. Jackpot Free Spins Feature Triggered by Yellow Eggs. During free spins, jackpot symbols (Mini, Minor, Maxi, Major, Mega) may appear. Each jackpot has a meter that fills as symbols land. When full, the jackpot is awarded and resets. At the start of the feature, jackpot values are randomly assigned within ranges. Mini Minor Maxi Major Mega 3x – 212.25x 10x – 219.25x 25x – 234.25x 150x – 359.25x 5000x Scatter Free Spins Feature Triggered by Pink Eggs. During the feature, Pink Eggs award fixed values when landing. All Pink Eggs share the same value during the feature. Values range from 1x to 7x and are randomly determined at the start. Game Rules Only the highest win per combination is paid. Malfunction voids all pays and plays. Visual elements such as animated characters are for entertainment purposes only and do not affect outcomes. Free spin features cannot be retriggered or combined with other features. RTP: 94% ANY WAY™ Pays Winning combinations consist of one symbol per reel from left to right. Multiple matching symbols on the same reel form separate combinations. The maximum number of winning ways is 720. Turbo Mode Turbo Mode speeds up gameplay and can be enabled or disabled at any time. Turbo Mode is automatically disabled when a Free Spins feature begins.
