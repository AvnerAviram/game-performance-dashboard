Three Bandits: Shoot 'n' Spin Game Information Three Bandits: Shoot ’n’ Spin Buy Bonus is a western-themed video slot featuring 5 reels, 4 rows, and 20 paylines. Game Features: • Multiplier Feature • Shoot 'n' Spin Feature • Jackpots Spin Feature • Feature Combinations • Buy Bonus Feature Gameplay Mechanics In the base game, each spin brings you closer to triggering special features. The Dynamite symbol acts as a Wild, substituting for most symbols to help form winning combinations. Bucks symbols appear in different colors and are used to fill corresponding vaults. When enough Bucks are collected, they may randomly trigger special features. Multiplier Feature Triggered by collecting Red Bucks, this feature awards 10 Free Games. During the feature, golden bullets may appear and shoot into the reels, transforming symbols into Wilds or Multiplier Wilds to increase winnings. Shoot 'n' Spin Feature This feature also awards 10 Free Games and is triggered by collecting Bucks. Players receive bullets (single shots or cylinders worth 5 bullets) used to hit targets and collect values. A Target Bounty counter rewards: • 5x total bet for hitting 25 targets • 100x total bet for hitting 30 targets Jackpots Spin Feature Triggered by collecting Blue Bucks, this feature starts with 10 Free Games. Only keys appear during this feature. Collecting 5 matching keys unlocks jackpots: Jackpot Payout Mini 5x total bet Minor 10x total bet Maxi 50x total bet Major 500x total bet Grand 2000x total bet The Skeleton Key counts toward all jackpots, increasing the chance of unlocking multiple prizes in a single feature. Feature Combinations Multiple features can be combined for enhanced gameplay: Shoot 'n' Spin + Jackpots Spin: Collect ammo and keys to win instant cash and jackpots. Shoot 'n' Spin + Multiplier: Use bullets to collect wins while golden bullets add multipliers. Multiplier + Jackpots Spin: Transform symbols into Wilds while collecting keys for jackpots. All Features Combined: Experience full gameplay with bullets, multipliers, and jackpots active together.
