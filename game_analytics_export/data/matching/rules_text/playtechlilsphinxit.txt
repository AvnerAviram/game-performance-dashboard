Lil Sphinx How to Play Press the play button on the entry screen to enter the main game. Open bet settings using the coin button and adjust your total bet using the plus or minus controls. Use the lightning bolt button to access the menu and activate Turbo Mode for faster gameplay. Press it again to disable Turbo Mode. Press the Spin button to start the reels. During spinning, the button changes to stop, allowing you to skip the animation and instantly display results. Winning spins display accumulated winnings in the win field. Wins are triggered by landing three or more matching symbols on consecutive reels from left to right on a payline. Special symbols such as Cash Prize Coin and Special Prize do not follow standard payline rules. Only the highest win per payline is awarded, while wins across multiple paylines are added together. All wins are calculated according to the paytable. Autoplay Autoplay allows reels to spin automatically for a selected number of rounds. Choose the number of spins and start autoplay from the menu. The number of remaining spins is displayed during autoplay. Autoplay stops when the selected spins are completed, balance is insufficient, or a feature is triggered. You can stop autoplay at any time using the stop button. Paytable Access the paytable using the information button. The paytable provides details on payouts, features, and game rules. Close the paytable using the back button to return to the game. Paylines The game features 40 fixed paylines. Winning combinations must start from the leftmost reel and follow a single payline across consecutive reels. Matching symbols must appear on adjacent reels along the same payline. Wild Symbol The Lil Sphinx symbol acts as the Wild symbol. It substitutes for all symbols except Cash Prize Coin and Special Prize symbols. The Wild symbol appears in the Cat Zone, typically on reel 3, row 2. When Extra Bet is active, Wild symbols can appear in additional Cat Zones on reels 2 and 4. Landing a Wild collects all visible Cash Prize Coin and Special Prize values. Wild symbols may also contribute to triggering Free Games by adding coins to pots above the reels. Cash Prize Coin Symbol Cash Prize Coin symbols display cash values and award them when collected by the Wild symbol. Values typically range from 0.5x to 1.5x the total bet. When Extra Bet is active, values adjust slightly but remain based on the total bet. Special Prize Symbol Special Prize symbols award fixed prizes such as Mega, Major, Minor, or Mini when collected. Prize values are calculated as multiples of the total bet. Values differ depending on whether Extra Bet mode is active. Rewind Feature If the Wild symbol does not land in the Cat Zone, there is a chance the reels may rewind to place it there. Additional rewinds may introduce Special Prize symbols into view. When Extra Bet is active, additional Cat Zones may also trigger rewinds. Free Games Feature Free Games can be triggered when the Wild symbol adds coins to pots above the reels. The more coins collected, the higher the chance of triggering Free Games. Each triggered pot awards six free spins. During Free Games, reels may be enhanced to guarantee Coin or Special Prize symbols. Additional Free Games can be retriggered indefinitely. Extra Bet Mode Extra Bet mode activates additional Cat Zones and increases gameplay opportunities. This mode increases the total bet by 25%. Payout values remain unchanged. Return to Player The RTP in the base game is approximately 93.88%. With Extra Bet mode active, RTP is approximately 93.90%. Disconnection Policy If disconnected during a spin, results will be displayed upon reconnection and winnings will be credited. If disconnected during a feature, the feature resumes after reconnecting. Autoplay spins complete automatically, but further spins will not continue.
