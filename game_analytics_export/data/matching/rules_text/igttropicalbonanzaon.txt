OVERVIEW The goal is to obtain 8 or more matching symbol anywhere in view across the reels. 2 How to Play Note: If you do not have enough money to play a game, the bet can be set to the minimum selectable amount through a dialog window informing about this. Wins are awarded when 8 or more matching symbols land anywhere in view, only the highest win per symbol is paid. Winning symbols are removed and new symbols will fall down to fill the gaps and create potential new wins. Winnings on different clusters are all added. Winnings from Bonus Spins feature are added to the total win. The auto spin feature does not automatically start Bonus Spins feature - player input is required. Tumble Feature After every spin in the Tumble Feature the winning combinations are paid and all winning symbols disappear. The remaining symbols fall to the bottom of the screen and the empty positions are replaced with symbols coming from above. Tumbling will continue until no more winning combinations appear as a result of a tumble. There’s no limit to the number of possible tumbles. All wins are added to the player’s balance after each tumble win. 3.2. Happy Hour Modifiers Randomly in the game on any paid spin the Happy Hour Modifier can trigger and award the following: Boost - Can randomly fill the cocktail glass to trigger Bonus Spins. Fruit Multiplier - Randomly a Fruit Multiplier can land with a value of between x2 and x20. The value of the Fruit Multiplier will be added to the total win of that specific spin. Possible Fruit Multiplier values (during base game) can be: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 8x, 10x and 20x. Only one Fruit Multiplier can be added by the feature. Mr Crab At any point during the base game Mr Crab can appear to signal a Big Win of 20x total bet or more, he can even signal the Bonus Spins trigger. 3.3. Bonus Spins Feature Landing 3 Bonus symbols anywhere in view will trigger the Bonus Spins Feature and award 8 Bonus Spins. During the Bonus Spins, the flow for winning symbols will be the same as the base game, 8 or more of the same symbols anywhere will trigger the Tumble Feature. On each Bonus Spin, 1, 2 or 3 Fruit Multipliers will land on the reels. Fruit Multipliers can land initially or from winning tumbles. Fruit Multipliers will be added together and applied to the total win of that spin. Possible added Multiplier values are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 8x, 10x, 20x, 30x, 40x, 50x, 60x and 100x. 3.4. Extra Bonus Spins During the Bonus Spins Feature any Bonus symbol that lands will award an Extra Bonus Spin (Max 5 per feature). 3.5. Cocktail Tumbler Trigger After each win and the Tumble Feature deploys, a section of the Cocktail will gradually fill in the glass. If 6 Tumbles are completed in a paid spin, the glass will fill and trigger the Bonus Spins Feature and award 8 Bonus Spins. At the end of a paid spin the if the Cocktail has not been filled, the glass will empty and reset the feature. The Cocktail Tumbler Trigger is not available in the Bonus Spins Feature. 3.6. Tropical Bet The Tropical Bet costs 75x Total Bet(1,500 coins). Tropical Bet is guaranteed to trigger the Bonus Spins Feature and award 8 Bonus Spins. The Tropical Bet will guarantee 3 Bonus symbols with no other winnings! 3.7. Extra Juice Side Bet Activate the Extra Juice Side Bet to increase your chance of triggering the Bonus Spins Feature. Extra Juice Side Bet will add 25% to the cost of each spin. Activating the Extra Juice Side Bet will not increase the paytable. 4. Other game buttons 4.1. Auto spin feature The loss limit setting allows stopping the auto spin feature automatically when the selected limit, plus your cumulative winnings, and minus your cumulative bets, is less than 0. 5.1. Desktop version Click the AUTO SPIN button to open the AUTO SPIN settings window where you can set up the following: Number of auto spins : click + or - to select the number of auto spins to play.
