More Chilli More Chilli Mega Pay Feature The MORE CHILLI Mega Pay Feature is triggered when 3 or more Scatter symbols appear. It can also be activated randomly at the end of any wagered game. When triggered, 15 Free Games are awarded. Press the PLAY button or tap the feature banner to begin the feature. During the feature, 2 sets of reels appear, and Free Games are played simultaneously on both sets. Collect Feature During Free Games During Free Games, Collect symbols contribute to the CHILLI METER. Accumulating Collect symbols unlocks additional reel sets and Wild transformations: Collect Count Feature Effect 9–13 Collects 3 sets of reels are activated. All symbols on reel 5 become Wilds across all reel sets. 14–29 Collects 4 sets of reels are activated. All symbols on reels 4 and 5 become Wilds across all reel sets. 30+ Collects 4 sets of reels are activated. All symbols on reels 3, 4, and 5 become Wilds across all reel sets. All symbols forming a winning combination must appear within the same reel set, including Scatter symbols. The MORE CHILLI Mega Pay Feature cannot be retriggered during the feature. During the feature, up to 4 reel sets can generate wins simultaneously. Each reel set plays and pays independently but uses the same Coin Size. Game Rules The game is played with 25 paylines. Each spin costs 40 coins per line, and the total bet is calculated as 40 coins multiplied by the selected Coin Size. Wins are awarded only on active paylines, except for Scatter wins, which pay regardless of position. Wins on different paylines are added together, but only the highest win per line is counted. Scatter wins are added to line wins, with only the highest Scatter win paid. Return to Player The theoretical Return to Player (RTP) for this game is 95.55%. RTP represents the percentage of total wagered money that is expected to be paid back to players over time. Malfunction Voids all pays and plays. Last modification date: 4/7/2026
