Captain Swashbuckle: Triple Collect Symbols The game includes various symbols with different payout values based on combinations. Higher-value symbols offer larger rewards for matching combinations, while lower-value symbols provide smaller payouts. Special symbols may also trigger features or bonuses within the game. Triple Collect Feature One or more coins may land on any spin and will be collected into the matching treasure chest. When a coin is collected, one of four features may be awarded: Chest 1, Chest 2, Chest 3, or Chest 1 & 3. Any of the chest features can be triggered on any spin. A combination of features can trigger enhanced rewards. Cannonball Cash Landing three scatter symbols in the base game triggers Cannonball Cash. Scatter symbols appear on reels 1, 3, and 5. Once activated, cannonballs will be fired onto the screen. Clicking or touching them awards cash values. Filling the on-screen meter awards the Buried Treasure Bonus. Selecting a treasure chest grants either a cash prize or the Jackpot Picker Bonus. Clicking cannonballs is for entertainment purposes only and does not affect outcomes. Booty Loot Free Spins Eight free spins are awarded by opening the relevant chest. Special collect and coin symbols appear during this feature. Landing a collect symbol gathers all visible coin values. Total wins include both line wins and collected coin values. This bonus cannot be retriggered. Booty Bounty Lock and Spin This feature is awarded by opening the relevant chest. Players start with three lives. Three positions are randomly assigned multiplier frames. Landing a coin locks it in place and resets lives to three. The bonus ends after three consecutive spins without a coin. All collected coins are summed and paid at the end. Coins landing in multiplier frames are multiplied, and multipliers may move or combine. Coin values range from 0.2x to 100x, and multiplier frames can be x2, x3, or x5. Captain's Jackpot Picker This feature is awarded by opening the relevant chest. Players pick coins until three matching values are found, awarding the corresponding jackpot. This bonus cannot be retriggered. Unlimited Big Booty Loot Free Spins Awarded when specific chests are opened in the same round. The feature continues until six coins are locked, triggering the Lock and Spin bonus. All locked coins carry over into the next bonus. This feature cannot be retriggered. Fortune Features Additional bonus features are available via the Fortune Features button. Double Treasure Ante Bet — Guarantees Unlimited Free Spins if Chest 1 or 3 is triggered. Increases bet by 50%. Cannonball Chaos Ante Bet — Doubles Cannonball Cash rewards at a cost of 2x the base stake. Mystery Bonus — Instantly awards one chest feature for 50x bet. Big Booty Bonus — Instantly awards Unlimited Free Spins for 100x bet. Super Mystery Bonus — Awards enhanced features when Double Treasure is active for 75x bet. 25 Paylines All 25 paylines are always active. Total bet equals 25 times the bet per line. Wins are evaluated left to right on adjacent reels. Only the highest win per line is paid. Wilds substitute for other symbols but must combine to form wins. Malfunctions void all pays and plays. Game Rules This is a 5-reel slot game with 25 paylines, all active by default. Winning combinations are formed by matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right. Only the longest matching combination is paid. Bets are locked once a spin starts. Winnings are paid in the player’s currency. Theoretical RTP is 94.13%. If interrupted, the game round may resume upon restart or be voided per policy.
