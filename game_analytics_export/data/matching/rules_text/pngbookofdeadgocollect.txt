Book of Dead GO Collect Book of Dead GO Collect Unfinished Games If your game round is disrupted, all game information and placed bets are stored until you reopen the game. You may continue your unfinished round from the point of interruption. Unresolved bets placed but remaining undecided in incomplete games will become void after 7 days. Malfunction voids all pays and plays. Information Payline wins occur on the number of selected paylines, according to the information in the paytable and game rules. When winning on multiple paylines in a single game round, all winnings are added together. All winnings from Features (such as Free Spins), Bonus Games and/or Scatters (if applicable) are also added to payline wins. All winning combinations are paid out at the end of a game round. Free Spin features are played with the same bet and same number of selected lines as the game round that triggered the feature – unless otherwise stated. The bet cannot be changed during a currently running game round. Please refer to the game rules for more information. All wins pay left to right, beginning with the left-most reel. In FREE SPINS only: Special expanding symbols will pay for any winning combination with gaps allowed. The wins pay from left to right. beginning on any reels where a winning combination is possible. Game Rules Book of Dead GO COLLECT is a 5-reel, 3-row video game with 10 paylines. All wins pay left to right on adjacent reels except TOMB SCATTER and EXPANDING SYMBOL wins which pay anywhere. Winning combinations begin with the left-most reel. Only highest win paid per selected line. TOMB SCATTERS TOMB SCATTERS can land on all reels. It’s also WILD that can substitute for all symbols, and it uses the highest payout value of the highest paying symbol. Landing 3, 4, or 5 TOMB SCATTERS anywhere pay X2, X20, or X200 of the total bet. TOMB SCATTER wins are multiplied by the total bet and are added to the payline wins. 3 or more TOMB SCATTERS will trigger FREE SPINS! FREE SPINS 10 FREE SPINS are awarded initially. Landing 3 or more TOMB SCATTERS will re-trigger 10 more FREE SPINS. When triggered, a symbol except TOMB SCATTER will be selected randomly. The selected symbol will become EXPANDING SYMBOLS in FREE SPINS. EXPANDING SYMBOLS pay anywhere. FREE SPINS are capped at 80. GO COLLECT Look for GOLD STATERS attached on all symbols except TOMB SCATTERS. The more GOLD STATERS collected in the base game, the higher the chance to trigger the TREASURE VAULT. GOLD STATERS cannot appear in FREE SPINS. TREASURE VAULT Starts with 3 RE-SPINS, landing STATERS will reset the RE-SPIN. Regular STATERS are X5, X4, X3, X2, and X1 of total bet. BONUS STATERS are GRAND X500, MAJOR X100, MINOR X20, and MINI X10 of total bet. After consuming all RE-SPINS, it awards the sum of all STATER values that landed times total bet. RE-SPINS will end when the reel has no more spaces for STATERS or if all the RE-SPINS are consumed. TREASURE GAMBLE Get a chance to double your winnings up to 4 times! Play GAMBLE allows you to enter a round where you must land more BRILLIANT STATERS than BROKEN STATERS. Landing more BRILLIANT STATERS will double your winnings while landing more BROKEN STATERS will wipe it all to 0. It is not possible to land the same count of BRILLIANT STATERS and BROKEN STATERS. COLLECT allows you to keep all your current winnings. This has a winning probability of 50% (1 in 2 chance) to double your total win from TREASURE VAULT and the line wins of the same round before TREASURE VAULT was triggered. TREASURE GAMBLE can be played a maximum of 4 times in succession and up to a limit of 5000x the total bet. TREASURE GAMBLE will not impact the overall RTP. If Auto Play mode is not disabled and TREASURE VAULT triggers, TREASURE GAMBLE is skipped and will not play. All the winnings from TREASURE VAULT and the line wins from the same round before TREASURE VAULT was triggered are automatically collected and the player will go back to the base game. The average RTP is 94.20%. RTP is achieved through participation. Bets are selected using the bet buttons. Click the plus and minus buttons to change the bet one step at a time. To start the round, click SPIN. When the reels stop, the symbols displayed determine your prize according to the paytable. Minimum Bet: 0.10 USD Maximum Bet: 50.00 USD Actions Paytable - Toggles the display of the paytable. Auto Play - Click the AUTO PLAY button to enable/disable the Auto Play feature. In Auto Play mode, a number of consecutive game rounds are initiated automatically using your current bet settings. The Auto Play mode is automatically disabled depending on your settings or if your balance becomes too low. Hyper Spin - Pressing and holding down the SPACE button activates Hyper Spin mode after 1.5 seconds. The spins speed up immensely but slow down if a feature is activated. Spin / Start / Spacebar - Starts the game round with the currently selected bet. Press Spin to begin. Stop - Stops the reels more quickly. Fast Play - Toggle on for a significantly faster gameplay. Auto adjust bet - Automatically reduces the total bet when starting a new spin, if the balance is too low to initiate a spin at the current bet level. The reduced total bet selected will be the highest possible bet but will still be lower than the selected bet size when autospins were initiated.
