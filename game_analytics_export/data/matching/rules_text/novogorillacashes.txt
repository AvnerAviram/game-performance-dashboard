Gorilla Cash Game Information Gorilla Cash is a jungle-themed slot game featuring 6 reels and 5 rows with an Anywhere Pays mechanic. Wins are determined by the total number of matching symbols appearing anywhere on the screen. Game Features: Coin Stack Collector Free Games Free Games Coin Stack Multiplier Gameplay Mechanics The base game consists of 6 reels, each with 5 rows. Wins are awarded when at least 8 matching symbols appear anywhere on the screen. Scatter symbols pay when 4 or more are visible. Winning symbols are removed after a win, and new symbols tumble down to fill empty spaces. This continues until no further winning combinations are formed. There is no limit to the number of tumbles in a single spin. Coin Collector Feature At the start of a round, reels may contain collector positions. If coin stack symbols land in these positions, their values are collected and added to the player's winnings. Coin values range from x0.25 to x1.5 of the current bet, while high-value coins range from x5 to x100 of the current bet. Buy Bonus The Buy Bonus feature can be activated during the base game. When triggered, it guarantees at least 4 Scatter symbols, awarding Free Games. The cost of activating the Buy Bonus is 50x the base game total bet. The paytable remains unchanged. Free Games Landing 4 or more Scatter symbols awards 10 Free Games. During Free Games, landing 4 or more Scatters awards an additional 5 Free Games. Collector positions remain locked throughout Free Games. Coin stack symbols may include multipliers that increase the Coin Multiplier by +1 when collected. The multiplier is applied to the total collected coin value at the end of each tumble. The Coin Multiplier starts at x1 at the beginning of each Free Games feature. Symbol payout values adjust according to the selected bet. Wins from different symbols are added together. Symbols pay regardless of position (Anywhere Pays system). Scatter symbols trigger Free Games when 4 or more appear. Free Games are played at the same bet as the base game. Multiplier symbols during Free Games increase the Coin Multiplier. The Coin Multiplier applies only to collected coin values per tumble. The Coin Multiplier resets to x1 at the start of each Free Games feature. Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
