No Stress Multiplier Kick back, relax, and let the wins roll in with No Stress Multiplier — your tropical ticket to rewards! Set on a serene island paradise with turquoise waters, white sands, and friendly turtles, this game delivers pure chill with powerful features. The Collection Feature gathers up multipliers from the reels, while the Lock and Spin Feature holds your winning symbols in place and keeps the action going. Take a deep breath, spin the reels, and let the island vibes guide you to fortune in No Stress Multiplier! General Information Table Game Type Video Slot Number of Reels and Rows 6x4 Return to Player 93.17% Minimum Multiplier Guarantee Mode RTP 93.17% RTP was calculated using math model on 10,000,000,000 rounds. Game Features COLLECTION FEATURE Collection symbol is active on the reels during the base game. Turtle symbols have attached stake multipliers: x0.5, x1, x1.5, x2, x2.5, x3, x4, x5, x7.5, x10, x20, x25, x50, x75, x100. Collection symbol on the reels collects all the multipliers from all turtle symbols on the reels. Turtle symbols lock on the reels for one paid spin. The reel state is saved separately for each stake and does not carry over to other stakes. LOCK AND SPIN FEATURE Lock and Spin feature Is triggered by 3 No Stress symbols in view. Turtle symbols lock on the reels until the end of the feature. Feature starts with 3 re-spins and each time a turtle symbol lands on the reels, the re-spins counter resets to 3. When no re-spins left or full board is filled with turtle symbols, all multipliers are collected from turtle and the feature ends. Collection symbol is not active during Lock and Spin feature. Lock and Spin feature starts after clicking the “Start” button on the Lock and Spin feature pop-up or after time out. MINIMUM MULTIPLIER GUARANTEE MODE Minimum Multiplier Guarantee mode doubles the stake. In this mode, small multipliers (x0.5, x1 and x1.5) cannot land on the reels. The available multipliers on the reels: x2, x2.5, x3, x4, x5, x7.5, x10, x20, x25, x50, x75, x100. Feature Respin™ Feature Respin™ is available after Lock and Spin feature. It gives a choice to take the winnings from Lock and Spin or to replay the feature. In case of choosing replay, winnings from Lock and Spin are burned. Feature Respin™ is available after Lock and Spin and can be played only once. The best strategy is to replay Lock and Spin when the win amount in Lock and Spin is less or equal than: -For base game: x46.50 stake -For Minimum Multiplier Guarantee: x61.65 stake Otherwise the best strategy is to take win. Choosing the Best Strategy does not guarantee that it will award a higher return. Gamble Enter Gamble feature via “Gamble” button after any winnings awarded during the game. Choose a colour or suit to play the Gamble feature. A card is dealt. Each card is drawn from a new 52 card deck. The gamble amount is doubled if the correct colour is chosen or quadrupled if the correct suit is chosen. If incorrect, the gamble is lost. Press Collect button to exit the gamble feature. You can gamble up to 5 times or until the take win amount is greater than $250,000.00. No more than $250,000.00 can be won from the gamble feature. The gamble feature is not available when the game winnings are greater than $125,000.00. The gamble feature will return an average payout of 100%. Please note that this does not mean that every play or series of plays will return this percentage. The outcome of each gamble is random and is unaffected by previous results. The Gamble enables/disables in the settings by the Gamble toggle. Bet Chance Once a player’s credit becomes less than the last spin stake amount the bet chance is activated. The dial becomes active. The odds of a successful bet chance are represented by the green segment. Landing on the green is a successful bet chance, red is a losing bet chance. On pressing SPIN the pointer will spin. If landing on the green, a spin of the reels is awarded at the same stake level that was used prior to bet chance activation. If red is landed, then credit is taken. When pressing cancel button the screen changes from the bet chance to the game, without any deduction from the remaining balance. The Bet chance disables/enables in the settings by the Bet chance toggle. Playing the Game Stake To play a round, configure your bet using the + / - buttons. Spin Options Click the Spin button or press the Spacebar to spin. The Spacebar disables/enables in the settings by the Spacebar to spin toggle. To play rounds faster, the player can use the Turbo option. Turbo – the reels spin and stop faster with no extra animation. Turbo can be activated by holding the Spacebar or turning it on from the Burger Menu. Payouts Rules Symbols pay reflects current bet configuration. Maximum win is limited to $250,000.00. Malfunction voids all pays and plays. If a disconnection from the game or a malfunction of the game occurs and you restart the game, your balance is reset to the amount before the interruption occurred. All open sessions, which are not concluded by the player, plus any future transactions for those sessions, will be terminated after 90 days maximum and a minimum of 7 days depending on the residence of the player. Please be aware that each account allows only a single game to be played at any one time. Therefore, a game should not be played on more than one device, or multiple games on the same device simultaneously. Doing so may lead to various errors. For the best gaming experience, use of the latest version of software is recommended.
