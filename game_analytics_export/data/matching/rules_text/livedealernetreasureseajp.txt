Pirates' Plenty: Treasures of the Seas Special Symbols and Features Wild Symbol Wild symbols appear on reels 2, 3, and 4. They substitute for all paying symbols and count as the symbol that forms the highest-paying combination on a win line. Map Symbols and Treasure Row Map symbols may appear during base gameplay and advance progress along the Treasure Row. Each Map symbol moves the ship forward by one step toward three locations: Plunder Bay, Port Royal, and Treasure Cove. Reaching these locations requires 10, 15, and 20 Map symbols respectively, unlocking Treasure Chests. Reaching Treasure Cove also adds a Treasure Spins symbol to reel 1. Treasure Chests require a specific number of Wilds in a spin to open and trigger respins with added features. Chest 1 requires 3 Wilds and removes low-paying symbols. Chest 2 requires 6 Wilds and removes card symbols. Chest 3 requires 9 Wilds and applies a 2x multiplier. Free Spins Landing three Free Spins symbols on reels 1, 3, and 5 awards 3 Free Spins. During Free Spins, each reel position acts independently. Wild symbols lock in place and reset spins to 3 when they land. If no Wild lands, there is a chance spins are reset by a random feature. Bonus features from Treasure Chests remain active throughout Free Spins. The feature ends after three consecutive spins without a new Wild. Treasure Spins Landing a Treasure Spins symbol on reel 1 and Free Spins symbols on reels 3 and 5 awards 3 Treasure Spins. Wilds are added to reels 1 and 5, and all positions act independently. Wilds lock and reset spins to 3 when they land. Additional Treasure Chests are available during this feature. Chest 4 requires 12 Wilds and applies a 5x multiplier. Chest 5 requires 15 Wilds and awards Kidd’s Treasure, ending the feature. The feature ends after three spins without a new Wild or when all positions are filled with Wilds. Kidd's Treasure Kidd’s Treasure is awarded when all five Treasure Chests are opened during Treasure Spins. This boosts the total win to 20,000x and ends the round. Game Progress Progress features, including locked symbols, are saved based on the selected stake. Different stake levels maintain separate progress states. Progress is preserved even when free spins are triggered via promotions. Feature Buy Feature Buy allows instant access to bonus features after confirmation. The cost depends on the selected stake and is shown before purchase. Refreshing during a feature returns the final result. Feature Buy does not contribute to jackpot winnings. Winning a jackpot involves spinning a wheel to determine the prize tier. Jackpots are not affected by multipliers and are awarded independently. Time Jackpot The Time Jackpot must be won before a specified deadline. The closer to the deadline, the higher the chances of winning. Once awarded, a new jackpot cycle begins. Game Rules All symbol values represent multipliers of the total stake. Wins are calculated according to the paytable. Line games pay based on paylines, while ways or cluster games pay based on combinations. Scatter wins are awarded in addition to other wins. Only the highest win per combination is paid.
