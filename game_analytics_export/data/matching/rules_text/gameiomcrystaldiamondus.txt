Crystal Diamond Crystal Diamond Shine bright with Crystal Diamond, a classic slot experience enhanced by five powerful multiplier wild symbols. These wilds substitute for Seven and Bar symbols and can multiply payouts by up to 5x. When two or more wilds land on the reels, their multipliers combine to increase winnings by as much as 20x. Wild symbols also unlock four jackpot tiers, giving every spin the potential for significant rewards. Wild Feature The Wild Feature introduces five unique multiplier wild symbols to the reels. These wilds substitute for Seven and Bar symbols and can multiply payouts by up to 5x. When multiple wild symbols land on a non-jackpot winning spin, their multipliers combine to create enhanced rewards, reaching up to 20x. Jackpot Wilds Feature Wild symbols also provide access to four jackpot tiers. Landing a mixed combination of wild symbols awards the fourth-tier jackpot. Landing a combination of 3x, 4x, and 5x wild symbols triggers the highest-tier jackpot. Different jackpot tier combinations are independent and do not substitute for each other.
