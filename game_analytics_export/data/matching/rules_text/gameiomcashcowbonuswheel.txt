Cash Cow Bonus Wheel Cash Cow Bonus Wheel is a feature packed slot game with the potential for significant wins. Landing one or more Dollar symbols can randomly trigger the Wheel Feature, where the number of Dollars determines how many pointers are placed on the wheel. The player then earns the credit values corresponding to the pointers they land under. The Wild Feature combines wild symbols and multipliers, boosting non-jackpot wins by up to 9x. Landing three Wild symbols triggers one of four Jackpot tiers, offering even greater rewards.
