Wild Symbol The Wild symbol substitutes for all symbols except Cash Collect and Cash Prize symbols. Only the highest win is paid per winning combination. Cash Collect Feature Cash Prize symbols appear on reels 1 and 3, while the Collect symbol appears on reel 2. If a Collect symbol lands together with Cash Prize symbols, the Cash Collect Feature is triggered. All Cash Prize values are accumulated on the Collect symbol. Fire Blitz Bonus The Fire Blitz Bonus is triggered when Cash Prize symbols land on reels 1 and 3 along with a Collect symbol on reel 2. The Collect symbol remains locked during the feature and gathers all Cash Prize values. The player starts with 3 spins, and each new Cash Prize symbol resets the spins back to 3. Up to 3 Collect symbols can appear simultaneously. The feature ends when no spins remain, awarding the total accumulated value. Triple Fire Feature Cash Prize symbols contribute to Fireballs displayed above the reels, which can trigger feature upgrades. Collect symbols contribute to all Fireballs and may activate multiple upgrades at once. Purple Fireball (Boost) Cash Prize values are boosted by 1x to 5x the base stake. High-value symbols may be upgraded to the next tier, except for the highest value. Red Fireball (Reels) This feature increases the reel height to 4 rows, providing more positions for symbols. Green Fireball (Multiplier) Multipliers are applied to positions, up to x3 per position per spin. Multiple multipliers on the same position are combined. Power Play Power Play allows wagering at 5x the base stake. Only Cash Prize and Collect symbols are active during this mode. The Triple Fire Feature remains active, and Fire Blitz guarantees at least one upgrade. Players can adjust stakes or return to standard mode at any time. Winlines The game is played with 5 active paylines. Autoplay Autoplay allows automatic spins at the selected stake. Players can set the number of spins and define stop conditions such as loss or win limits. Game Rules The total bet can be adjusted before spinning. Winning combinations are formed from left to right on consecutive reels. Wins on multiple paylines are added together. Only the highest win per payline is awarded. Scatter wins are paid in addition to regular wins. Cash Prize values range from 1x to 1000x the stake. General Information The Fire Blitz Bonus is played using the base game stake. Feature upgrades are applied in the order: Green Multiplier, then Purple Boost. Gameplay Controls Press the Spin button to start the game. Wins are evaluated from left to right. Only the highest win per line is paid. RTP and Version Standard Play RTP: 92% + 2% Jackpot + 1% Reserve. Power Play RTP: 92% + 2% Jackpot + 1% Reserve. Game version: 1.7.0.2_hotfix_6.
