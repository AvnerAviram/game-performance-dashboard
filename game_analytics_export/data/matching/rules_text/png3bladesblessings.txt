3 Blades and Blessings Unfinished Games If your game round is disrupted, all game information and placed bets are stored until you reopen the game. You may continue your unfinished round from the point of interruption. Unresolved bets placed but remaining undecided in incomplete games will become void after 7 days. Malfunction voids all pays and plays. Information Payline wins occur on the number of selected paylines, according to the information in the paytable and game rules. When winning on multiple paylines in a single game round, all winnings are added together. All winnings from Features (such as Free Spins), Bonus Games and/or Scatters (if applicable) are also added to payline wins. All winning combinations are paid out at the end of a game round. Free Spin features are played with the same bet and same number of selected lines as the game round that triggered the feature – unless otherwise stated. The bet cannot be changed during a currently running game round. Please refer to the game rules for more information. All wins pay left to right, beginning with the left-most reel. Symbol wins pay for any winning payline combination without any gaps. Only the highest win is paid per active win line. Game Rules 3 BLADES & BLESSINGS is a 5-reel video game with 20 paylines by default. During FREE SPINS, the number of paylines can increase to up to 40, depending on the reel size. SPECIAL SYMBOLS This game features special symbols: WILD and SCATTERS. The WILD symbol substitutes for all symbols except for SCATTER symbols. There are five SCATTER symbol types: Green, Purple, and Red Coins in the Base Game, and Prize Coin and BONUS Coin during FREE SPINS. During the Base Game, only one Coin (Green, Purple, or Red) can land on each reel and is collected in its corresponding chest above the reels. The Green, Purple, and Red Coin symbols are collected in the COLLECT, BOOST, and BONUS chests, respectively. Collecting Green, Purple, or Red Coins, alone or in combination, may randomly trigger FREE SPINS. The number of Green, Purple, and Red Coins collected does not affect the chance of triggering FREE SPINS. The Prize Coin and BONUS Coin symbols can appear once on any reel. The Prize Coin symbol appears only during the COLLECT FREE SPINS feature and in combined FREE SPINS with COLLECT mechanics. This symbol contains prize values of 10x, 5x, 2x, and 1x. These values are multiplied by the total bet. On the other hand, the BONUS Coin symbol appears only during the BONUS FREE SPINS feature and in combined FREE SPINS with BONUS mechanics. This symbol reveals either GRAND, MAJOR, MINOR, or +1 FREE SPIN when it appears on the reels. COLLECT FREE SPINS Collecting Green Coin symbols in the COLLECT chest may activate the COLLECT FREE SPINS feature and award 7 FREE SPINS. During the feature, Prize Coin symbols can appear on the reels, with their displayed prizes instantly collected in the collection panel and awarded when the feature ends. The feature cannot be retriggered. BOOST FREE SPINS Collecting Purple Coin symbols in the BOOST chest may activate the BOOST FREE SPINS feature and award 7 FREE SPINS. During the feature, the reels are expanded to 5 reels and 5 rows with 40 paylines. All payline wins are doubled during this feature. The feature cannot be retriggered. BONUS FREE SPINS Collecting Red Coin symbols in the BONUS chest may activate the BONUS FREE SPINS feature and award 7 FREE SPINS. During the feature, BONUS Coin symbols can appear on the reels and reveal either GRAND, MAJOR, MINOR, or +1 FREE SPIN. Collecting 3 GRAND BONUS Coin symbols awards the GRAND BONUS of 500x. Collecting 3 MAJOR BONUS Coin symbols awards the MAJOR BONUS of 50x. Collecting 3 MINOR BONUS Coin symbols awards the MINOR BONUS of 20x. All values are multiplied by the total bet when awarded. An extra FREE SPIN is added to the total when a +1 FREE SPIN is revealed. The maximum number of FREE SPINS is 50. SUPER FREE SPINS Collecting two Coin types in their respective chests and triggering two FREE SPINS at once activates the SUPER FREE SPINS feature and awards 7 SUPER FREE SPINS. There are three types of SUPER FREE SPINS feature, determined by the combination of the two FREE SPINS features that triggered them: COLLECT + BOOST, BOOST + BONUS, and BONUS + COLLECT. All mechanics from the two combined FREE SPINS features are applied during the feature. Only payline wins are doubled when the BOOST mechanics are active during the feature, excluding Coin prizes and BONUS prizes. The number of SUPER FREE SPINS can be extended when BONUS mechanics are active during the feature. MEGA FREE SPINS Collecting three Coin types in their respective chests and triggering all FREE SPINS at once activates the MEGA FREE SPINS feature and awards 7 MEGA FREE SPINS. All mechanics from the three combined FREE SPINS features are applied during the feature. Only payline wins are doubled during the feature, excluding Coin prizes and BONUS prizes. The number of MEGA FREE SPINS can increase during the feature, as the BONUS mechanics are active during the feature. The average RTP is 94.20%. RTP is achieved through participation. Bets are selected using the bet buttons. Click the plus and minus buttons to change the bet one step at a time. To start the round, click SPIN. When the reels stop, the symbols displayed determine your prize according to the paytable. Any combination of wins resulting from a single game cycle, including the results of any possible triggered bonuses, is capped at a maximum of 4000 times total bet. Auto adjust bet - Automatically reduces the total bet when starting a new spin, if the balance is too low to initiate a spin at the current bet level. The reduced total bet selected will be the highest possible bet but will still be lower than the selected bet size when autospins were initiated. The bet may only be decreased and will not return to its original amount following any wins that would allow the initial bet size to be resumed.
